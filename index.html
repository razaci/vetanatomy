Anatomical directions: how to read the model

Cranial — toward the head. Caudal — toward the tail.

Dorsal — toward the back. Ventral — toward the belly.

Medial — toward the midline. Lateral — away from the midline.

Proximal — nearer a limb's attachment to the body. Distal — farther from it.

Thoracic — chest region. Abdominal — belly region. Pelvic — rear cavity region.

Right & left — always the dog's own sides, regardless of how the picture faces you.

On this side drawing the head points right and the back is above the belly.
